Getting started

Signing in

Your dashboard is one link, one email and one password. That's it.

  1. Open the dashboard link the REPS team sent you.
  2. Enter your email and password, then press Sign in.
  3. You'll stay signed in on that device for 30 days.
Forgotten your password? Just message the REPS team and we'll set you a new one.

What's in the menu

  • Leads: everyone who's filled in a form on your site, and where they are now.
  • Website: your site, where you leave comments and approve designs.
  • Emails: your launch emails, to read and approve.
  • Tasks: your to-dos from our calls.
  • Meetings: notes from every call with us.

Leads

Your leads board

Every person who fills in a form on your site lands here automatically. No copying, no spreadsheets.

The board: your numbers along the top, then one column per stage.

The columns

New leadThey've just filled in a form on your site.
NurturingThey're getting your emails.
Call bookedThey've booked a call with you.
Showed / No-showThe call time has passed.
PurchasedThey've paid. The amount shows on the card.
LostNot going ahead. You move them here yourself.
Cards can move by themselves. Once your booking calendar and payments are connected, a card moves to the right column when someone books a call or pays.

Looking at a lead

Click any card to see everything about that person on the right.

Answers are kept separately for each form they filled in.

  • Their details: name, email and phone, ready to contact.
  • Their answers: what they told you, under each form's name. Did your quiz and your calculator? You'll see both.
  • Move manually: tap a stage to move their card yourself, e.g. to Lost.
  • Timeline: everything they've done, newest first.
  • Notes: anything you want to remember about them. Only you and the REPS team can see these.

Tap a stage to move a lead yourself.

Comments and changes

Want something changed? Point at it and tell us, like sticking a note on the page.

1. Click the spot. A numbered pin marks it.

2. Type what you'd like and press Post.

  1. Press + Add comment.
  2. Click the exact spot on your site: a headline, a photo, a button.
  3. Type what you'd like changed and press Post.
  4. Add as many as you like, on any page, on desktop or mobile.
  5. When you're done, press Request changes. That sends them all to us at once.
Be specific. "Change this to: Your next level starts now" is quicker for us than "make this better".

Your comments, listed on the right. Click one to jump to it.

What happens next

We go through every comment and mark each one Done by REPS or Not changed. If we don't change something, we leave a short note saying why. Once we've been through them all, your site comes back to you as Ready for review.

Approving your design

Happy with everything? Press Approve design, then confirm. That's your sign-off, and we'll take it from there to put it live.

Not quite there? Leave more comments and press Request changes again. There's no limit.

Quick answers

Do I need to refresh to see new leads?New leads show whenever you open Leads. If the page has been open a while, refresh it to see the latest.
Someone filled in two forms. Are they in twice?No. It's one card, with the answers from each form inside.
Can I undo a comment?Yes. Press Resolve on it before you request changes and it won't be sent.
Why are the buttons greyed out?You've sent changes and we're working on them. They come back once we're done.
Can my team have a login?Yes. Ask us and we'll set one up for them.
Something not right?Message the REPS team any time. We're here to help.
